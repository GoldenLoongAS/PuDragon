Unleash the power of nuclear energy!

Pu Dragon introduces the Plutonium Dragon – a new, fully-fledged dragon species for the Dragon Survival mod. Forged deep within uranium-rich veins, this radioactive dragon radiates deadly green energy and brings an entirely unique nuclear-themed playstyle.

---

## 🔥 Core Features

- **Living Nuclear Reactor** – Naturally immune to radiation, the Plutonium Dragon thrives in environments that would be lethal to others. Its presence alters the world around it, making radioactive blocks and items hazardous to non-plutonium creatures.
- **Complete Growth System** – Evolve from a glowing Newborn to a towering Ancient dragon, with each stage unlocking new abilities, visual changes, and stat bonuses.
- **Rich Skill Tree** – Master a variety of nuclear-themed skills, including Nuclear Blast, Laser Guidance, Gravity Compression, Quantumization, Overload, and many more. Each skill offers unique combat and utility options.
- **Advanced Flight Mechanics** – Experience smooth, responsive flight with camera effects, speed boosts, and trail particles that make flying feel natural and thrilling.
- **Custom HUD & GUI** – Dedicated interfaces display radiation levels, skill status, target locking, and more, providing full control over your dragon’s abilities.
- **Enhanced Visuals & Performance** – Improved special effects (magic circles, shockwaves, auras) and optimized performance for a polished experience.
- **Full Compatibility** – Works seamlessly with JEI (for recipe viewing) and other mods.

---

## 📦 Dependencies

- **Required**: Dragon Survival (≥2.0.53)
- **Required**: GeckoLib (≥4.6)
- **Optional**: JEI (≥19.21) – for recipe integration
